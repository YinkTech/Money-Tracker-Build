# Product Scope

## Target audience

Freelancers who want a clearer picture of the money they earn and spend.

## Problem statement

Freelancers may receive income at different times and need to keep track of both earnings and expenses. Without organized records, it can be difficult to see where money goes or understand spending habits.

## Proposed solution

A money-tracking app that lets freelancers record their income and expenses, organize transactions into categories, and review spending by category.

The app helps users understand their finances. It does not hold, transfer, or invest their money.

## Core MVP features

- Record income and expenses.
- Assign a category to each transaction.
- View a summary of income, expenses, and balance.
- Review transaction history.
- See spending totals grouped by category.

## Features excluded from the MVP

- Stock and bond tracking, comparison, or recommendations.
- Investing or locking funds through the app.
- Bank integrations or moving money.
- Loan and debt management.
- Wealth-creation lessons.
- Inventory management.
